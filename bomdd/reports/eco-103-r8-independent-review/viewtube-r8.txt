### R8 — Independent review before user golden

Any product-source change receives a fresh-context independent review. Classify all findings.
Open in-scope findings block golden and return to R5. Out-of-scope findings follow R3. Record the
review identity, findings, and disposition in the ECO evidence.

### R9 — Risk-weighted impact closure before golden

Beginning with ECO-VT-055, UI and UI-inspection changes covered by
`change-impact-policy.yaml` carry a versioned semantic change-impact manifest. Inventory the
changed semantic parts and their direct/dependent consumers before probe manufacture. At the
`implemented` transition every required consumer must have operating evidence; unresolved and
deferred required work must both be zero. A fresh-context reviewer reconstructs the boundary and
reviews exclusions independently from the manufacturer.
